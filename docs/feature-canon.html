Lessons from the churn

What keeps getting lost

  • Fixing past rounds. v3 had a round strip; since v4, a score can't be corrected once the 5-second Undo has gone.
  • Coaching depth. Tips went from two beginner tips plus two pro tips per round to a single line.
  • The tablet. v3's side rail and two-column layout became a centred phone column.
  • Share fallbacks. v6 dropped the clipboard fallback, so Share does nothing on some browsers.
  • Looks as worlds. Looks with their own fonts and art were reduced to colour swaps.

Bugs to carry forward as fixed

  • Live sync never retries a failed push and has no reconnect listener.
  • Removed players show as "?" in past games.
  • Re-counting a hand starts from an empty keypad, and scanned suits are thrown away.
  • Haptics do nothing on iPhone (navigator.vibrate), so feedback needs a visual or sound fallback.
  • The AI SDK was loaded without a pinned version, and the API key sat in plain localStorage.
  • The tip-jar link is still a placeholder.

A local play-test lab

The proposal: before any build is called done, it goes through the same panel. Seeded games drive the real app in a local browser at phone, tablet and TV sizes. Then a reviewer for each lens scores what it sees against a fixed rubric. Every lens runs the same four players.

Personas every lens uses

  • Gran, first game. Large text, outdoors, has never seen the rules.
  • The host. Keeps score on one phone for six players, fast and impatient.
  • The competitive teen. On their own phone, wants stats and bragging rights.
  • Family night. A tablet propped up as the scoreboard, phones joining by code.
