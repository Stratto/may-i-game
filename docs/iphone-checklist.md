# iPhone checklist, 24 Nov 2026

About 20 minutes on a real iPhone, using the live site (https://stratto.github.io/may-i-game/) from the Home Screen. Each check says what to do and what good looks like. If something looks wrong, a screenshot and the check's number is all I need.

**Before you start:** open the app from the Home Screen, close it, and open it again, so it picks up today's version.

## 1. Back and swipe back
1. Open a table from Play, then swipe back from the left edge. *Good:* you're back on Play.
2. On a table, open **Table settings**, then swipe back. *Good:* the sheet closes and you stay on the table.
3. Start a game and swipe back during a round. *Good:* the round stays, and "Still playing" shows once.
4. Finish a game, then swipe back on Results. *Good:* you land on that table.

## 2. This week's changes
1. **Sheets:** open any sheet and close it, then open one and drag it down. *Good:* both slide down smoothly, not vanish.
2. **Score pad:** count a hand with the cards, then tap **Use N**. *Good:* the next empty hand is ready to type in, without the page jumping.
3. **Rematch:** play a game with one of a table's regulars left out. *Good:* Results shows "Name sat out · Add back", and Add back changes the button to "Rematch with N".
4. **Stats:** *Good:* "Nearly there" lists people with a bar, and a tap opens their profile.
5. **Tip cup** (Settings): *Good:* the cup has tea and steam rising.
6. **Background lights:** *Good:* they drift very slowly on Play and in a round, and stand still with Calm on.

## 3. Things only a real phone shows
1. **The black square:** look at the top-left corner of Results and Learn. *Good:* no black square. (It shows in the test browser, so this is the real test.)
2. **Zoom on typing:** tap a name field and a score box. *Good:* the page doesn't zoom in.
3. **Icons and cursor:** *Good:* every icon shows (no empty boxes), and the text cursor sits in the field you tapped.
4. **Battery:** leave the app open on Play for 10 minutes. *Good:* the phone doesn't get warm and battery drops no faster than a web page would.

## 4. Larger text
Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size → Larger Text, one step up from the default, then open the app.
*Good:* names, scores and buttons grow, nothing is cut off, and the round still fits four players without scrolling past the main button.

## 5. Sunlight
Turn on **Sunlight contrast** in Settings, then take the phone outside or turn brightness right down. Try it on a table in each look (Lagoon, Game night, Lime, Cherry).
*Good:* scores and names are easy to read; nothing is pale grey on white.

## 6. VoiceOver
Settings → Accessibility → VoiceOver on (triple-click the side button turns it off again, if you set that shortcut).
1. Swipe through Play. *Good:* each table and button is read with a clear name.
2. In a round, swipe to a face. *Good:* it reads the name, the total and buys.
3. End a game without saving. *Good:* VoiceOver moves to **Undo** straight away.
4. On Results. *Good:* it reads the winner, then places, then scores by round.

## 7. Offline
Play a round, then switch on Airplane Mode and close the app fully. Open it again.
*Good:* it opens, the game is where you left it, and you can score the next round. Turn Airplane Mode off after.
