# Design: May I? · "Lagoon"

Mode: Operate. A premium family card game in your pocket: deep teal water under a faint suit pattern, cream paper sheets, enamel buttons in gilt rims, and 3D icons for the moments that matter. Warm and crafted, never a casino. The reference for every screen is the kit at `docs/ui-kit.html`; this file records what has been built into the app (`index.html` holds the styles and SVG sprite, `src/ClaudeApp.jsx` the React app).

**Rollout complete.** Every screen was rebuilt from the kit in five phases: (1) foundations, (2) Home and New game, (3) table, score pad and between rounds, (4) finale and results, (5) Learn, Stats, profile and cabinet, Settings, dressing room and Players. Lagoon, Game night, Lime and Cherry are mapped onto the kit's materials (`MAPPED_LOOKS`); Daylight shows Lagoon until it is mapped too.

## Looks
Looks belong to tables (owner's call, 22 Nov). A table stores its look as `group.look`: a new table is dealt one that no other table is wearing yet (`dealLook`), and tables made before then show Lagoon (`tableLook`). It can be changed in the table's settings sheet (four tilted tiles, one tap, no Save). The table's page, its games, score pad, between rounds and results wear its look, and its card on Play takes the look's colour (`LOOK_TILE`). Play, Learn, Stats and Settings always stay on Lagoon. There is no light mode; the Sunlight contrast switch covers bright rooms. The four mapped looks are below; Daylight is not offered. `applyLook()` writes the look tokens as CSS variables on `<html>` before the first paint and follows the system dark mode. A new device starts on Lagoon.

Every mapped look uses the same materials: the water (the ground layer with two orbiting lights and the suit tile), enamel buttons and bars in gilt rims, a jewel on the main button, paper cards and sheets, and gold trim. Gold and paper never change. Per look, the CSS redefines the material tokens near the end of the sheet (`:root[data-look=…]`, then `[data-scheme=light|dark]`).

In light mode the water is pale, panels are white and text is dark ink; highlights on the water use the look's deep gold (`--hl`). In dark mode the water is deep, panels are translucent like Lagoon's and highlights are cream gold. The tab bar, Playing now card, toasts and the between-rounds veil stay deep enamel in both modes, with cream text.

**Material tokens per look:** `--g4` / `--g45` / `--g5` (water), `--orb1` / `--orb2` (orbit lights, RGB triplets), `--tile` (suit tile), `--halo1` / `--halo2` (glow behind the wordmark), `--teal-*` (enamel), `--sap-*` and `--sap-deep` (May I?), `--gem-1…3` (jewels and sparks), `--well-*` / `--well2-*` (inset wells and switch tracks), `--dis-*` (disabled buttons), `--panel-*` / `--tab-*` / `--panel-ink2` (deep panels), `--now-*` / `--again-*` (Playing now card), `--hl` (highlight text on the water), `--gline` / `--gline2` (hairlines and dashes on the water), `--glass` / `--glass-ink` (rounds still to come), `--track` (award race lanes), `--scrim`, `--veil`, `--glow` / `--glow2` (between rounds).

"Surprise me" rolls through the looks, and "New look each time" opens the app on a different one every launch.

## Colors
**Lagoon materials** (static, in the CSS `:root`): water `--g1`…`--g5` (`--g5` #0B5866 is the ground), gilt `--gold-hi` #FFEDB0 / `--gilt` #E9B44C / `--gold-lo` #A86F1B / `--gold-deep`, rims `--rim` (gilt gradient) and `--rim-soft`, teal enamel `--teal-hi` … `--teal-deep`, sapphire `--sap-*`, ruby `--ruby-*`, paper `--paper-hi` #FFFBEF / `--paper` #FBF0D6 / `--paper-lo` #F1DDB3, paper ink `--paper-ink` #0F3247 / `--paper-ink2` #3D5361, suits `--heart` / `--spade` / `--club`, and `--lift` for floating trays. On the water: text #FFF7E2, secondary #D9F3EC, quiet #A9D6CD, accent gold #FFE7A3, panels rgba(3,38,46,.5) with gold hairlines rgba(255,233,166,.18). Inside a sheet the look tokens are redefined for paper: ink #0F3247, accent deep gold #8B5A12, red #A8263B, out #0E6A55, focus teal.

**Look tokens**: `--bg`, `--surface`, `--fill`, `--line`, `--ink` / `--ink2` / `--ink3`, `--band` / `--band2` / `--band-ink`, `--accent` / `--accent-ink` / `--accent-soft`, `--cta` / `--cta-hi` / `--cta-edge` / `--cta-ink`, `--gold` / `--gold-ring` / `--gold-soft` (leader only), `--out` / `--out-soft` (went out), `--red`, `--focus`. Text pairs meet 4.5:1 in every look. Peg colours (red, blue, green, amber, purple, teal, pink, orange) identify a player for life and never mean anything else. Gold, silver and bronze appear only on crowns and podium blocks.

## Typography
Three faces from Google Fonts plus the system face. **Fraunces** (`--display`, 600–900, SOFT 50 on buttons and titles) for large titles (800, 2.3rem), sheet titles, row titles, button labels, segmented options and the share image. **Barlow Condensed** (`--label`, 700–800, uppercase, wide tracking) for list section headers, the back label, tab labels, ON/OFF, sync and Undo. **Barlow Semi Condensed** (`--num`, tabular figures) for scores and numbers. System SF (`--font`) for body copy and secondary lines, with Dynamic Type through `font: -apple-system-body`. No eyebrow labels above headings: the caps labels head lists, never titles.

## Shape and depth
**Buttons** (`.btn`) are enamel pills in a 2.5px gilt rim with a gem stud at each end: 52px (`.lg` 56px, `.sm` 42px). Primary (`.primary`, `.gold`) is teal enamel; second (`.tonal`, `.on-band`) is cream paper; destructive (`.tonal.danger`) is ruby with ruby gems; quiet (`.plain`) is gold words with a soft underline. The depth is all inside the shape, a soft raised inner edge (`inset 0 -5px 0` in a deeper tone plus a 2px top highlight), and pressing sinks the button 2px. No outer, offset or zero-blur shadows on buttons. Buttons side by side sit 22px apart so the gems never touch. Disabled is a greyed teal with soft rims.
**Round icon buttons** (`.nav-icon`, `.nb-ic`, `.round-btn`) are 44px teal discs in a gilt rim, cream on paper. **Lists** are dark glass panels, radius 20 with a 1px gold hairline border, 64px rows, Fraunces row titles, and leading icons in 40px rimmed teal discs. **Sheets** are paper with a 3px gilt rim, radius 26. Floating trays (tab bar, toast) carry `--lift`. Avatars on colour get a 3px white rim and a short soft shadow.

## Components
- **Tab bar:** a gilt-rimmed dark teal tray floating over the water, with 3D icons (`art/icons/play`, `rules`, `trophy`) and Barlow caps labels. The chosen tab's icon lifts and brightens, and a gold diamond pip slides beneath it (`--i` on `.tabbar-in`). Play, Learn and Stats on the three top-level screens. Learn holds the whole guide (rounds, basics, Q&A, house rules). Stats is the trophy room: who has won each award most often across all games, and every table's leader. Pushed screens (a table, a game, results) have a back button and no tab bar.
- **Page:** a large Fraunces title that collapses into a blurred 60px nav bar after 44px of scroll. Back is a round rimmed button followed by the destination in gold caps; the right side holds up to two round icon buttons.
- **Segmented control:** a dark trough in a soft rim; one paper pill glides to the choice (`--i` and `--n` on `.seg`). On paper the trough is light and the pill is teal.
- **Switch:** 78×38 slot that turns teal, a paper knob with a gilt edge, and ON or OFF in the slot.
- **Check and deal chips:** rimmed squares that fill with teal; caps chips that turn cream on the water and teal on paper.
- **Table nav (`.tnav`):** on the water, no band. Back with the group name; on wide screens a screen icon (scoreboard); ••• (this game: Finish now and count, End without saving, Look/sounds/scanner).
- **Contract:** a paper card in a gilt rim: "Round n of 7 · Ana deals 12" in caps, the contract name, small kit cards (clubs green, hearts and diamonds red). The rules book sits in a cream circle at the top right; the whole card is one button into the rules.
- **Turn order (the round's players):** replaces the oval table (owner, 22 Nov: it did not show the order and did not scale with the count). Players are listed in the order they play: 1 is whoever goes first, and the dealer comes last with a paper "D" in place of a number ("Deals 10" beside their pips). Each row is a dark glass button with the number, the face, the name, a diamond pip per allowed buy and, once a round is scored, the running total (gold for the leader, who also wears the crown). On phones, up to four players sit in one column and five to eight in two, read down the left column and then the right. The heading row is dropped on phones so the round fits at 390 by 664. Tablets show one wide column for up to four and two columns for more. Tap a row to record a buy; a toast in place of the hint line takes it back. While scoring, tap a row to open that player's score.
- **Dock:** a single primary button for the next step: Score round n, Keep scoring, Start round n, or Finish game · see who won (the contract name waits for the intro card). A round never moves on by itself. If nobody (or more than one player) is out, the dock says Mark who went out.
- **Checking a round:** once every score is in, the seats become a 2-column hand of paper player cards dealt in: avatar, name, "+25" or "Out", "Total 40", and a cream pencil to change that score. Whoever went out sits on a gold card that hops, throws crystal sparks and gets the out stamp.
- **New leader:** when the last score of a round hands the lead to someone else, a banner toast drops a crown: "Ben takes the lead!" with the margin under it (`toast.art`, `toast.sub`).
- **Score pad:** the who-strip (40px avatars; the chosen one on a white card in a gilt rim; a saved score lands with a pop, and an Out throws small sparks), a plaque with the big number (an Out shows the stamp), Type points / Tap cards / Photo, then the pad. Keys are cream with a pressed edge; delete is teal. Tap cards: keys sit five to a row with each card's points under it, the last row sharing the width; the hand fans in a dashed tray and overlaps tighter as it grows, each new card dealing in; tap a card to take it out. Footer: "Ana went out" (one per round; on someone else it reads "Out instead of Ben"; gold when on; nudges when everyone else has points) and Save · next player. Fix round n walks every player and closes on Done.
- **Players in this game:** "Add or remove" in the seats header. Add someone from the table or a new name; missed rounds count as each round's highest score (or zero). Removing someone acts at once with Undo. Whoever is dealing keeps the deal.
- **Between rounds lines:** the heading names who went out ("Ana went out", or "Nobody went out"). Each standing has at most one line, and a line never repeats the total beside it. After round 1, when every total is that round's score, lines are words: In the lead, Lightest hand, Close behind, Got caught, Biggest hand. A line appears once; later rows that would say the same thing stay quiet. The leader's line never uses the crown art, because their face already wears it.
- **Between rounds:** a blurred lagoon glow, a "Cy won round 4" pill with the trophy, then the next contract's card dealt face down (the deck back, sized to the card exactly) and turned over: corner indices in the round's suit, "Round 5 of 7", the suit jewel, the name, the melds, "Ben deals 12 cards", the tip, and a gold line that runs down before play starts by itself (5.7s). Undo round n and Let's play.
- **Results (finale):** a full-bleed stage under the nav (`Page` `top` prop), deep enamel (`--now-2` to `--now-3`) in every look and both modes, so the fireworks always have a night sky. Slowly turning gold rays, the trophy, the winner on an enamel ribbon with tails (the page's h1), the lowest score and date, then a podium (2, 1, 3): avatars rise, enamel plinths in gilt rims grow with medal art, and the crown drops onto first place. A Replay pill under the base bar. On a fresh finish (or Replay) fireworks burst into rings and card suits in random colours behind the trophy, and confetti with the jewel suits falls once, then a lighter drift loops; both sleep off screen. Below: table moments (the first as a paper plaque with the table's avatars, any others in one paper list), Everyone else, Awards (paper cards with an enamel medal, the four rarest first, then All N awards), the race, what got people caught, round by round, reopen and delete. Share (nav bar) makes a 1080×1350 image in the current look.
- **Round strip:** seven cream mini cards (44×58, gilt edge). The current one (`.rs.cur`) is lifted, scaled and gold-rimmed with "Now" under it; rounds to come are dashed glass; a finished round wears a dot in the colour of whoever went out (a check if nobody did) and opens "Fix round n". Never name a class `.now`: that's Home's Playing now card and its rules leak.
- **Reopen:** a finished game's results offer Reopen to fix scores; it moves back to Playing now on its first unfinished round.
- **Finish now and count:** in the game menu once a round is done. Only finished rounds count, the results say "after 3 of 7 rounds", and Undo is offered.
- **Play (returning):** tables lead (owner's call, 22 Nov). A game in progress shows first as the Playing now card; then "Tables" with New table; then Last game. Each table is a full-width enamel card (teal, sapphire, ruby, jade in turn) showing the name, who holds it ("Ana holds it · 2 wins"), who won last ("Fin won last · today"), up to four faces, and the champion's card with the crown. Tapping the card opens the table. A cream **New game** pill on the card opens New game for that table. The table the dock button names ("New game at Friday night") has no pill, so the same action never appears twice.
- **New game:** the main button under Home's hand: teal in every state, the same on first run and once a game is playing (the Playing now card carries Resume). The sheet shows your tables as enamel blocks (teal, sapphire, ruby, jade in turn, with the suit tile; a table that's playing resumes) and a dashed New players block that opens the player editor, Who deals first and house rules. With no tables yet, the editor is open from the start.
- **Undo toast:** a deep teal banner in a gilt rim with a cream Undo pill. Ending, finishing, deleting a game and deleting a table act at once and show a toast with Undo for 6 seconds. Nothing asks twice.
- **Round guide (Rules sheet and Learn):** tabs Rounds, Basics, Q&A, House. Rounds are enamel medallions (the chosen one lifts). A round is a paper card in a gilt rim with its number on an enamel medallion at the top, the contract line in caps, kit cards, the goal and three chips (dealt, in contract, spare or fit in runs). Under it, paper callouts with an enamel medallion each (`Call`): Watch out (ruby), After you go down, Tips, and Pro tips (open for seasoned players, or a Show pro tips button). Basics: numbered steps in enamel rings, and the points left in hand as tilted paper tiles. Q&A and the glossary are paper panels with gilt hairlines.
- **Coaching level:** asked once after the first deal ("Have you played before?"). Learners get the round guide at the start of every round. It can be changed in Settings, where it is two paper choices with art (I'm still learning, I know the game).
- **Settings:** the looks as tilted tiles, three across, each its own water with its jewel (Lagoon's crown, Game night's spade, Lime's club, Cherry's heart, Daylight's diamond) and an enamel tick on the chosen one, plus a parchment Surprise me tile that rolls. Then switches, coaching, the card scanner (camera art) and the tip link.
- **Card scanner:** Photo is a viewfinder with gold corners, a ghost fan of cards and the camera art. The photo develops in it and a light sweeps across while it reads; a failed read shakes and the corners turn red. Afterwards Tap cards shows a receipt (the photo as a stub, "Read 6 cards", what to check) above the hand, and guessed cards wear a dashed gold outline. Take photo and Choose photo are text-only. It uses the player's own Anthropic API key, stored on that device only; without one the key card sits under the viewfinder.
- **Hand coach:** at the bottom of the current round's guide. A private photo of your hand returns what Claude sees, what you have, what to aim for, keep, discard and one tip.
- **Still in it:** under the seats between rounds, one row per trailing player with the gap, the rounds left and the margin needed each round. Seats show "▲ up 2" or "▼ down 1" after each round.
- **Award race:** under the seats once two rounds are done: chips with each award's art (`AWARD_ART`), its title in gold, who would win it and their avatar. One per row on phones.
- **Table podium:** the table page hero shows the top three by wins on the finale's medal podium, smaller (`.fin-podium.tp`): 2, 1, 3, a crown on the leader, each tappable for their profile. With no games yet, the players' avatars sit beside the title.
- **Race track:** racing pegs on lanes filled with a pastel of each player's colour, with the lowest total nearest the chequered finish. It appears on the results screen, and fills the TV scoreboard on wide screens.
- **Awards (24):** one table, `AWARDS` (title, art, rarity, the rule in a line). The original six (Clean hands, Bargain hunter, Comeback kid, Steady hand, Heavy pockets, Joker juggler) plus Flawless, Hat trick, Last-round heist, Up from the bottom, Wire to wire, Photo finish, Run maker, Set collector, Self-made, Hot on their heels, Ace up the sleeve, Good sport, Friends in high places, Curtain call, Rollercoaster, Dealer's choice, So close and Quick off the mark. A "most" award needs a single leader (a tie gives none); a threshold award can go to several players; winner awards need a sole winner. Each line says what happened ("Went out in rounds 5, 6 and 7"). The award race at the table keeps the original six. **Table moments** (`tableMoments`): Shared crown, Twins, Nail-biter, Marathon and Packed table belong to the whole table.
- **What got people caught:** points by card type as bars, plus the most caught card, the toughest round and a heavy-buying warning. It needs counted cards.
- **Profiles:** tap a player on the podium or in the standings. The sheet opens on a stage in the player's colour where their card (or avatar) hops in, then the streak or wins line with the crown and a paper Dress up button, a dotted ledger of eight numbers at this table, the Cabinet (the four most recent things earned on a gold shelf, one button into the cabinet) and head to head.
- **Cabinet and Hall of fame (`Shelf`):** medallions on gold rails under a spotlight; tap one and it pops into the spotlight with its rule and status. Unearned ones wait greyed in dashed slots. The cabinet has two shelves: 17 lifetime achievements (`LIFE`, `lifeAchievements`, earned once and dated, across every table the same name plays at) and the 24 game awards at this table (×N pills). Stats shows the Hall of fame: who has won each award most often across every table (shared on a tie, holders' avatars on the medallion).
- **Lifetime achievements (17):** Crowned, Out of the gate, Beginner's luck, Grand tour, Full cabinet, Back to back, Dynasty, Payback, Record breaker, Untouchable, Nemesis, Social butterfly, Regular, Part of the furniture, Night owl, Early bird, Say cheese (a hand scored from a photo, whose cards keep their suits).
- **Heroes:** Learn, Stats and the table page open on a full-bleed top section under the nav (`.ptop`, from `Page`'s `hero` prop): the finale's deep enamel and glow, a gilt bottom edge, the title in cream, a line under it and art on the right (the rules book, the crown) or the podium. Home has its own: the wordmark (`art/logo-wordmark.webp`) as the page's h1, a hand of 7♥ 7♠ 7♣ and the Joker dealt from the deck in code over a soft teal glow, a one-line status and New game. The hand deals in with a stagger; a tap (or hover) spreads it and lifts the Joker.
- **Roll for dealer:** a quiet pill (die icon, caps) in the table's New game header. The dealer chip hops between players and slows to a stop while the die spins.
- **Sounds:** short WebAudio tones (tap, card snap, save, deal, out and a winner's fanfare), on by default with a switch in Settings. Haptics stay on regardless.
- **Avatars:** a player is one of the deck's own faces, a photo or their initials. There are no made-up characters.
  - **The faces:** 17 of them: the Kings, Queens, Jacks and Aces of every suit, plus the Joker. Courts and the Joker are head-and-shoulders crops of the deck's own art (`art/avatars/<suit>-<k|q|j>.webp` and `joker.webp`, 256px). Aces use the suit art (`art/suits/*.webp`), shown smaller and contained.
  - **At the table:** a face sits in a cream medallion ringed in the player's colour (`.peg.card`). Initials sit on the colour itself, and a photo fills the circle.
  - **New players:** each is dealt a face nobody at their table holds, picked from their name so it's the same every time. Only the 13 people are dealt; Aces can be picked but aren't dealt.
  - **Old pictures:** players with no picture, or an old 3D character, are dealt a face in seat order when the app shows them (`dealFaces`). Nothing is saved until the player picks one.
  - **Opening the dressing room:** from anywhere a player appears: tap a seat avatar during a game, tap Dress up on a profile (Save returns to the profile), or tap the hanger badge in Edit players.
  - **Inside it:** Cards / Photo / Initials sit at the top, and it opens on Cards.
    - A stage in the player's colour deals the picked card itself, tilted. The card hops in again with each pick.
    - A small circle in the corner shows the medallion the table will see.
    - A rail on the right holds Shuffle (die), which deals a different card.
    - Two tabs, Cards and Colour, open one grid each. Cards sit four across with their names, and the chosen one gets an accent ring. Colour swatches show the player's face on each colour.
  - **Photo:** Take, Choose and Paste (for Memoji stickers); saved as a 192px JPEG.
  - **Everywhere:** avatars show wherever a player does, including the share image. A profile with a card face opens on a tinted panel holding the real card above the Dress up button.
- **Sheets:** cream paper inside a gilt rim, with a cream round close button, a Fraunces title and a paper-toned footer. Bottom sheets on phones (swipe down, scrim, Escape), centred at 700px and up. Every overlay (sheets, the round intro, toasts, confetti) renders on `<body>`, so a sheet opened from a sheet always lands on top. The page behind is locked while a sheet is open, and sheets follow the visual viewport so the keyboard never covers a field.
- **Card points:** ace and joker points are their own house rules (default 15 and 15), separate from where aces sit in runs. Games from before this keep their old scoring.
- **Playing now:** a teal enamel card in a gilt rim. A small card tilted -6° shows the round number and the round's suit (heart, spade, diamond, club in turn); it straightens on hover. Then a pulsing live label, the table name, round and contract, and a foot with the players, a crown for the leader and a Resume pill. The whole card is the button. Play again (after a game) uses the same layout on deep teal with a soft rim, a card back and a cream Deal pill.
- **Deck cards (`DeckCard`):** the kit's illustrated deck in code: a 63 × 88 cream face with a double gold rule and corner curls (SVG symbols in the page's sprite), Fraunces indices with flat suit glyphs, true pip layouts with the illustrated suit art, ring-framed aces, double-ended court portraits and the Joker. Everything is sized in container units, so one card works at any width. Decorative unless given a `label`.
- **Player editor:** rows with a 46px avatar wearing a teal hanger badge (opens the dressing room), the name on a dotted line that turns into a rimmed field when focused, and a cream round bin. The add row is a dashed circle, "Add a player" and an Add pill. New table names use a labelled field with a soft rim.
- **Credits:** the end of Settings credits Fraunces (Undercase Type) and Barlow (Jeremy Tribby), both OFL.
- **Ground:** a fixed layer behind everything: a radial teal gradient, two soft lights orbiting slowly (still under reduced motion), and the suit tile (`art/suit-tile.webp`) as an 11% mask. Only `<html>` paints a colour, so nothing covers it.
- **Sharing (paused):** Share and Join are hidden until the sync server is switched on; no control leads to a dead end.

## Layout
Content max 720px. At 900px and up the game shows seats on the left and the round grid on the right, the Rules sheet lists the rounds by name beside the guide, and Home and the table page widen to 1040px in two columns: Home's hero spans both and sits side by side (wordmark, status and New game on the left, the hand on the right), then Playing now beside Tables, and Standings beside Records. Scoreboard mode fills the screen with the band and large seats.

## Motion
`--out-e: cubic-bezier(.16,.84,.3,1)` and `--spring: cubic-bezier(.34,1.3,.64,1)`. Seats rise in a stagger, totals count up, the intro card is dealt in with a spring and flips face up, May I? bumps and flies a card to the caller, points float up as they land, the out player's card hops with sparks, buttons press down, and the finale's fireworks and confetti play on a fresh finish or Replay only. Everything collapses under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Files and build
- `index.html`: the page, the kit styles (`<style id="kit">`, `<style id="kit-app">`) and the SVG sprite. It loads `src/main.jsx`.
- `src/ClaudeApp.jsx`: the whole React app, with rules, scoring and stats inside it. `src/main.jsx` mounts it and registers the service worker.
- `art/`: the one folder for images. The app asks for `art/...` at run time; the build copies it as-is.
- `public/`: icons, `manifest.json` and `sw.js`, copied to the root of the build. Each build gives the service worker a new cache name, so there is nothing to bump by hand.
- `npm run dev` serves on port 8743. `npm run build` writes `dist/`. Pushing to `main` runs `.github/workflows/pages.yml`, which tests, builds and publishes `dist/` to GitHub Pages.

## Testing
- `npm test` runs unit tests against the real app module (`src/ClaudeApp.jsx`): rules, scoring, stats and coaching content.
- `npm run e2e` (with `npm run dev` running) walks the app in Safari's engine at 390 by 664, an iPhone's Safari height. Every press is a real tap at the point the browser says is on top. It checks that every visible control can be tapped, that each tab works, that a House rules switch changes, and that the round fits without scrolling. Screenshots go to `tests/e2e/shots/`.
- `tests/lab.html` plays scripted scenarios at five sizes and checks for sideways scrolling, clipped text and tap targets under 44px.
- None of these replaces a real phone: check each phase once on an iPhone.

## Don't
Two controls for one action. Deep, offset or zero-blur shadows on buttons. Felt or velvet, poker terms, bets, coins, gems as currency, levels or rewards as currency: the gold and gems are trim, never money. Busy layouts. Anything childish. Gradient text. Eyebrow labels above headings. Modals where a sheet or inline state will do. Emoji icons in the interface. Fonts beyond Fraunces, Barlow and SF. Copying another game's branding, characters or art. Colour that isn't a material, a look token, a player or a state.
